It covers your use of <website link> (or its dedicated storefront) and describes how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you browse, order, or interact with the Store.

What Does the Anytime Astro Store Do? 

The Anytime Astro Store is an e-commerce platform, that allows users to book online Pujas and Astrology-based services, and to purchase gemstones, rudraksha, yantras, and other spiritual remedy products. The Store facilitates browsing, cart checkout, payment, order fulfilment, and delivery of physical and service-based products. 

Information We Collect 

1. Information You Provide to Us 

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you: 

Create an account or check out as a guest on the Store — name, email address, mobile number, and password (or OTP) for account security. 

Place an order for a Puja, gemstone, rudraksha, or remedy — billing and shipping address, phone number, and, where relevant to a Puja or remedy (such as gemstone recommendations or personalised rituals), birth details (date, time, and place of birth), gender, and Sankalp/family details you choose to share. 

Complete payment — your name, billing address, and payment method type. Card, UPI, or net-banking details themselves are collected and processed directly by our payment gateway; we do not store full card numbers on our servers. 

Contact customer support, request a refund/exchange, or ask a query — the details of your query along with your order ID and contact information. 

Subscribe to marketing emails, WhatsApp/SMS updates, or participate in a promotion, offer, or giveaway. 

Leave a product review, rating, or testimonial. 

2. Information Collected Automatically 

When you browse or shop on the Store, we and our service providers automatically collect certain information, including: 

Device and browser information — IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers. 

Usage data — pages viewed, products browsed, items added to cart, time spent on pages, and referring/exit URLs. 

Location data — approximate location inferred from your IP address, used to show relevant currency, shipping options, and offers. 

Cart and checkout behaviour — used to recover abandoned carts and improve the checkout experience. 

3. Information from Shopify and Other Service Providers 

As our Store runs on the Shopify platform, Shopify collects certain information on our behalf to operate the Store, process orders, and prevent fraud, in accordance with the Shopify Privacy Policy. Shopify may also independently use certain information to improve its own platform, as described in its own privacy documentation. 

You can review Shopify's data practices here: Shopify Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to operate the Store, and with the following categories of service providers: 

Shopify — as the platform provider hosting our Store, order, and customer data. 

Third party payment gateway — to securely process your payment. These gateways are PCI-DSS compliant, and we do not receive or store your full card or bank account details. 

Shipping and logistics partners — to deliver physical products (gemstones, rudraksha, remedy kits) to your address, and to enable order tracking.

Any third party that receives your information is contractually or legally bound to use it only for the purpose for which it was shared and to keep it confidential. 

Payments 

All payments on the Store are processed through Shopify Payments and/or third-party payment gateways. When you check out, the payment gateway may collect information such as your name, billing address, phone number, email, and card/UPI details directly. We receive only limited information (such as payment status and a masked reference) necessary to confirm and process your order. We do not store your complete card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers. 

Cookies and Tracking Technologies 

The Store uses cookies and similar tracking technologies (including those provided by Shopify, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel) to remember your cart, preferences, and login state, to understand how you use the Store, and to show you relevant offers and retargeting ads. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect certain Store features, such as cart persistence and saved login.

Your personal information is stored on secure servers maintained by Shopify and our other service providers, in an encrypted format where applicable. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, subject to the retention we are legally required to maintain (such as invoicing records). 

Your Rights and Choices
